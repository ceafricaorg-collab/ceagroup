FAQ

Common questions, answered

Everything property owners ask us before listing.

Does it cost anything to list my property?

Listing is free for our first 100 verified properties verification included, no hidden charges. Your spot is reserved the moment you submit your details above. After the launch period, a standard fee applies and you'll be notified well in advance.

How long does verification take?

Our team reviews title documents and confirms property details within 7 business days of receiving your full listing.

What documents will I need?

You'll need your title documents and a valid NIN or BVN for identity verification. We'll guide you through exactly what's required once you receive your secure link.

Is my information shared with anyone?

No. CEA will never share your details without your consent. Your information is used only to verify and list your property.

How are buyer payments protected?

Buyer funds are held in a CEA escrow account and only released to you once document transfer is confirmed.

What happens after I submit my details here?

You'll receive a secure, tokenised link by email (valid 48–72 hours) to complete your full property listing and verification.
