What We're Building

Our energy program is designed around three interconnected pillars that work with our agriculture and property networks.

☀
Solar Micro-Grids
Distributed solar installations powering farms, homes, and communities in off-grid and under-served areas across Nigeria.
♺
Bioenergy & Waste-to-Power
Converting agricultural waste into clean energy closing the loop between CEA's farming and energy operations.
⇋
Peer-to-Peer Trading
Blockchain-enabled energy trading letting communities buy and sell surplus power directly, cutting out middlemen.
🌿
Eco-Integrated Design
Every CEA property is designed with renewable energy built in solar panels, energy storage, and smart management systems.
