What We're Building

Our agriculture programme integrates soil health, renewable power, and market access into a single Econetwork that benefits every farmer it touches.

🌿
Climate-Smart Farming
Training and equipping farmers with drought-resilient varieties, soil-health monitoring, and water-efficient irrigation practices.
📊
Market Linkages
Connecting smallholder farmers directly to domestic processors and international export markets cutting out exploitative middlemen.
⚡
Solar-Powered Operations
Every CEA farm hub runs on renewable energy from our sister energy programme reducing costs and carbon emissions together.
📱
AgriTech Platform
A digital platform tracking yields, soil data, weather, and payments giving farmers and investors real-time visibility into farm performance.
