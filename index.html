About CEA Group

A New Vision for Africa's Sustainable Future

At Castlerock Econetwork Africa Group (CEA Group), we are reimagining how Africa grows by connecting its most vital sectors into one powerful, self-sustaining ecosystem. We're not just a company; we're a movement for integrated growth, where agriculture, renewable energy, property development, and technology work hand-in-hand to power communities, create jobs, and protect the planet.

Founded in 2024, CEA Group is a privately held multi-sector enterprise built to drive Africa's transition toward a sustainable and prosperous future. Our unique model, the "Econetwork", ensures that every project reinforces the next. Farms are powered by renewable energy. Green buildings are constructed using sustainable materials. Technology connects everything through data and innovation.

Our Mission

To design, deploy, and scale proprietary development models where our agricultural, energy, and property assets interoperate to generate compounding economic and environmental returns.

Our Vision

To transform the African landscape into a blueprint for sustainable civilization, built on the synergy of food, energy, and housing.

The Econetwork Model

Every project reinforces the next, creating a virtuous cycle of prosperity for communities, investors, and stakeholders.
